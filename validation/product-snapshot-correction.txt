A browser audit reproduced a hidden-output Shiny eventReactive defect:
click Calculate at effect .5, change effect to .8 while the result tab remains
hidden, then open results. The deferred calculation returned 26 per arm
(effect .8), instead of 64 per arm for the clicked .5 assumptions.

Planning handlers now compute eagerly in observeEvent and store immutable
results/errors. The same change covers fixed sample size/power, sequential
boundaries/enrollment, survival planning and comparisons. Simulations already
freeze their projects in immediate job-launch observers. Regression tests check
parameters/counts after edits without requesting the original output first;
a browser regression explicitly keeps the tab hidden during parameter edits.
